Appellation Atlas

Privacy

Updated 21 November 2026. This covers the Wine Atlas app for iPhone and iPad and this website.

In short

The Atlas collects nothing about you. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you see is already inside the app or on this site.

Your location

The app asks for your location only when you tap the location arrow, Where am I. It uses it on your phone to find the wine place you are standing in. Your location is never sent anywhere or stored.

Saved places and notes

The places you save, and your notes on them, are kept on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple syncs them to your other devices through your own private iCloud storage. We cannot see them.

Settings remembered on your device

The app and the website remember a few choices on your device, such as whether Benchmarks or All is picked. They never leave your device.

Feedback

If you tap Send feedback, a Google Form opens in your browser with the place filled in. You choose what to write. Your email address is optional and is used only to reply to you. Answers go to the Atlas’s Google account, and Google’s privacy policy applies to the form.

This website

The site is hosted by Vercel, which handles each request for a page and may keep basic server logs under its own privacy policy. The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.

Links to other sites

Places link to Wikipedia and to the sources of their facts. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Questions

Ask through the feedback form. If this policy changes, this page will say so and show the new date.
