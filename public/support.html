Support

Help with the Wine Atlas app for iPhone and iPad, and this website.

Get in touch

Spotted something wrong or missing, or need help? Use the feedback form. On any place in the app or on the site, Send feedback at the bottom of the page opens it with that place filled in.

Common questions

Does the app need a connection? No. Every place, map and fact is inside the app.

How do saved places sync? Through your own iCloud account. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on each device.

Where am I, the location arrow, says I am outside every wine place. It only finds places the Atlas has a boundary for. Some places have no map yet.

Where do the facts come from? Every place lists its sources at the bottom of its page. About the Atlas lists the main data sets.
